Privacy Policy

Smoke Clipper is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed: permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to read the status of those videos (YouTube Data API: upload and read-only scopes). No other Google user data is read.
  • Use: uploading the owner's recordings as private videos and checking that YouTube has finished processing them.
  • Storage: the OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's own server and is never shared.
  • Sharing: no data is shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone.

Smoke Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
